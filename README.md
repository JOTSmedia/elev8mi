# ELEV8MI v25 — clear scenery and balanced celestial design

Open ELEV8MI-preview-v25.html for the complete offline preview.
Upload the CONTENTS of website/ to the GitHub repository root, replacing
matching files and including assets/ and vendor/. No build step is required.

## Changes
- Earth rotates once every 360 display seconds (six minutes): three times
  slower than v24. Its cloud/weather layers remain independently animated.
- Moon/daylight cycle remains 85.714 seconds; Earth display year remains
  17.5 seconds. The live lunar texture and night opening are retained.
- Hero emblem has a soft dark corona and a crisp edge for consistent contrast
  through sunrise, daylight, sunset, twilight and night. Its glow still pulses.
- Desktop/landscape devices use wide 1672 × 940 forest and cave compositions.
  Portrait screens retain 941 × 1672 compositions. Each scene has its own
  height; the scroll no longer stretches every scene to the space panorama.
  Water, cloud, tree and wildlife masks follow the selected composition.
- All seven small planet globes rotate on their axes, with Venus and Uranus
  retrograde. Higher-detail, interpolated surface sampling smooths rotation.
  Sun-facing highlights, cool lunar fill and Saturn ring incidence follow
  the displayed light positions. Small bodies keep a readable minimum size.
- Projected planet sprites separate at conjunctions, including Saturn's
  rings and the Moon. Mathematical orbit trajectories remain unchanged.
- Loader, navigation, audio, 78-card draws, reading selection and centered
  interpretation email dialog are preserved. Pause and reduced motion work.

Lighting, orbit distances, minimum visible planet sizes and time are cinematic
illustrations rather than a single-scale scientific model. Planet surfaces
use rotating illustrative photographs; only Earth uses global satellite maps.
Weather is animated historical imagery, not live local conditions. Moon phase
uses the device date. Credits, generation prompts and licenses are in assets/
and vendor/. The wider scenery is AI-refined photography, not a 4K claim.
