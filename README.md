# ELEV8MI v28

This is a new, complete ELEV8MI-v28/ folder. Previous versions are untouched.
Open ELEV8MI-preview-v28.html for the complete offline preview.
For GitHub Pages, upload the CONTENTS of website/ to your repository root,
replacing matching files. Include assets/, assets/cards/, vendor/ and all CSS/JS.

## This release
- Brighter Earth terrain, clouds, atmosphere, planet surfaces and stars.
- Solid gold nav trails circle the pill when idle, then land above/below the
  active desktop link; mobile frames the selected menu link in gold.
- An original acoustic-inspired fingerpicked instrumental is the default
  optional soundtrack. C5 is tuned to 528 Hz; the music contains many pitches.
  Cello, violin, piano and the pure 528 Hz tone are selectable in the dropdown. Play/stop and volume are explicit;
  audio never starts automatically and stops when the tab is hidden.
- TAP INTO YOUR ENERGY has one-card and three-card options, retaining the
  uppercase reading-style dropdown. Both prompt Allison's interpretation.
- A complete original 78-card SVG deck: 22 Major Arcana; Wands, Cups, Swords
  and Pentacles, each Ace–Ten, Page, Knight, Queen, King. Traditional identities
  and upright/reversed meanings remain, with original celestial brand artwork.
- Representative moons orbit Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune.
  They have shaded spherical surfaces and rear/front orbit layers. Mobile
  shows fewer satellites for clarity. Earth's existing lunar representation
  remains; Mercury and Venus have no natural moons in the display.
- Contact email: elev8miangel@gmail.com. Instagram: @elev8mi.
  The eventual www.elev8mi.com address is linked and marked Coming soon.

selected card PNGs inline, including their upright/reversed orientations.
Open it in a compatible desktop mail client, review and send. For webmail,
Save card pictures downloads a PNG to attach manually. Open email app uses

by itself. All 78 public PNGs are included under website/assets/cards/.
Recipient: elev8miangel@gmail.com. Subject: INTERPRET MY CARDS.

Nothing sends automatically.

## Rendering notes
Celestial sizes, distances and time are compressed for presentation, not a
single-scale scientific model. Representative moon periods are based on
https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/sats/elem/; the satellite display compresses them.
Earth surface rotation is six minutes; the Moon/daylight cycle is about
85.714 seconds. Historical satellite imagery is animated, not live weather.
The live lunar phase uses the device date. Pause/reduced motion are supported.
