How Allison reads

A reading here is not a fortune taped to the wall. It is a look at the stage you are actually in — appetite, stillness, or first flight — and a clean name for what comes next.

Sit with Allison in the room, or call in from wherever you are. Ask about available sessions and choose a format that suits you.

A little guidance before you draw

Choose a reading style from the dropdown: general guidance, relationships, career, personal growth, or another focus. Keep a specific question in mind if you like.

One card gives you a single theme. Three cards offer three perspectives: past, present, and what may unfold.

Press Shuffle & choose. Choose one or three face-down cards from the shuffled deck. Each selected card appears in your reading. Its meaning appears underneath once your spread is complete.

A reversed card is another angle on the theme, not a bad omen. Use the reading as a prompt for reflection, rather than a guaranteed prediction.
