# Original ELEV8MI Celestial Tarot

Version 28 replaces downloaded Rider–Waite–Smith scans with original SVG
illustrations in tarot-art.js. No external paintings, scans or emoji are used.
The deck contains 78 traditional identities and upright/reversed meanings:
22 Major Arcana and four suits with Ace–Ten, Page, Knight, Queen and King.
Counted pips, symbolic Major scenes, curved robes, angel wings and botanical
motifs are designed in purple, blue, pearl and gold for this website.
The Hanged One is the site's title for the traditional Hanged Man.
All 78 PNGs in cards/ are raster exports of these original SVGs for email links.
